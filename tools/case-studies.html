What is
Witbit Labs?

Witbit Labs is an AI-native automation studio. We build the systems that remove repetitive work from businesses — AI agents, system integrations, data pipelines and custom software — shipped by a small senior team that thinks in AI natively, not retrofitting it.

We work like an in-house team, not as vendors: discovery in days, working software in weeks, and continuous optimization after launch. The following pages document four things we built — for a German-language news operation, a 800K-follower creator, a SaaS learning product, and an influencer marketing agency.
