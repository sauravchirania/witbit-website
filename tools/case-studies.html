01 / Mobility / Uber × Metro
Connecting Urban Mobility Through Seamless Digital Ticketing
Building a unified mobility experience across five metropolitan cities.
The Vision

Urban mobility is evolving beyond individual transportation services. The future lies in connected ecosystems where commuters can seamlessly move between different modes of transport through a single digital experience.

Our team engineered the integration that enabled metro ticket booking directly through the Uber application, connecting Uber's platform with metro rail networks across five metropolitan cities.

What We Built
  • Metro ticket booking directly through the Uber application.
  • Seamless connectivity between Uber's platform and metro ticketing systems.
  • Integration with metro networks across five metropolitan cities.
  • Reliable backend infrastructure supporting high-volume commuter transactions.
  • Scalable technology designed to accommodate growing urban mobility demand.
The Impact
5 Metropolitan Cities
Connected metro networks across five cities through a unified digital mobility experience.
100,000 Daily Passenger Capacity
Infrastructure designed to support approximately one lakh commuters per day.
One Connected Mobility Ecosystem
Commuters access metro ticketing through an existing digital mobility platform.
Enterprise-Grade Integration
Multiple transportation systems connected into a seamless booking experience.
The Bigger Picture

This wasn't simply an application integration. It was the engineering of a connected mobility layer between a global digital platform and large-scale public transportation infrastructure — a foundation for more accessible, connected and scalable commuter experiences.

02 / Advertising / PhonePe
Engineering the Infrastructure Behind Digital Advertising
Building an advertising technology ecosystem for one of India's largest digital platforms.
The Challenge

Digital advertising at enterprise scale requires sophisticated infrastructure capable of managing advertising requests, audience targeting, campaign delivery, platform integrations and monetization — all while maintaining reliability across a high-volume digital ecosystem.

PhonePe required a robust technology system to integrate advertising capabilities and enable targeted advertising experiences within its application.

What We Built

During their time at PhonePe, our engineers developed and integrated advertising technology infrastructure designed to support:

  • Advertising system integration within the PhonePe application.
  • Meta advertising integration and campaign delivery capabilities.
  • Audience targeting and advertising placement infrastructure.
  • High-volume advertising request processing.
  • Backend systems supporting advertising operations and monetization.
  • Scalable architecture designed for continuous platform activity.
The Impact
100 Million Daily Unique Users
Technology integrated into a digital ecosystem reaching approximately 100 million daily unique users, as reported for the platform.
50,000 Daily Advertising Requests
Infrastructure designed to handle approximately 50,000 advertising requests per day.
₹1 Crore Daily Revenue
The advertising ecosystem was associated with approximately ₹1 crore in daily revenue.
Enterprise-Scale Integration
Advertising capabilities enabled within a high-traffic digital application.
The Bigger Picture

The work helped transform advertising capabilities into an integrated technology layer within an established digital ecosystem — building the underlying systems that make digital advertising operationally viable within a large consumer platform.

03 / Healthcare / Integrated Hospital OS
An Integrated Hospital Operating System for an IVF Network
Connecting clinical operations, financial workflows and pharmacy management through one technology ecosystem.
The Challenge

Patient billing, pharmacy operations, financial management and clinical workflows must work together accurately and efficiently. For an IVF clinic network, managing these systems independently creates operational complexity, fragmented information and inefficiencies across the patient journey.

What We Built

Our team developed an integrated hospital operating system designed to bring critical operational functions together:

Billing & Financial Management

  • Patient billing workflows, financial operations and integrated operational records.

Pharmacy Management

  • Pharmacy inventory workflows, medicine transactions and billing integration.

Healthcare Operations

  • Connected workflows across clinic functions with centralized architecture for a multi-location IVF network.
The Impact
Integrated Operations
Billing, pharmacy and financial management connected within a unified platform.
Operational Visibility
Centralized management of critical healthcare workflows.
Multi-Function Integration
Fewer disconnected systems across key operational departments.
Scalable Infrastructure
A foundation designed for the operational requirements of an IVF clinic chain.
The Bigger Picture

Healthcare technology is most effective when the systems behind patient care work together. By integrating billing, pharmacy and financial operations, the platform built an operational foundation to manage complexity while supporting more efficient clinic operations.

04 / Commerce / Tata 1MG
Intelligent Inventory & Demand Management Infrastructure
Engineering inventory intelligence for high-volume digital healthcare commerce.
The Challenge

Large digital healthcare platforms must continuously manage purchasing, inventory availability, demand fluctuations, replenishment decisions and operational forecasting. With approximately 20,000 daily orders, inventory management becomes a critical component of operational efficiency.

What We Built

Our team developed an integrated inventory management and planning system for Tata 1MG, designed to support:

Inventory & Purchase Management

  • Centralized inventory tracking, availability monitoring and stock management.
  • Purchase workflows, procurement management and replenishment planning.

Demand Forecasting & Supply Planning

  • Future demand prediction and inventory planning based on demand trends.
  • Demand-driven production planning and operational planning based on projected demand.
The Impact
20,000 Daily Orders
Technology supporting inventory workflows associated with approximately 20,000 daily orders.
Intelligent Inventory Management
Centralized inventory visibility and structured stock management.
Predictive Demand Planning
Forecasting integrated into inventory and purchasing workflows.
Smarter Procurement
Purchasing decisions supported through inventory and demand intelligence.
The Bigger Picture

Inventory intelligence is fundamental to operating high-volume commerce platforms. By integrating inventory management, purchasing, demand forecasting and production planning, the system was designed to help businesses move beyond reactive inventory management toward more intelligent operational planning.
