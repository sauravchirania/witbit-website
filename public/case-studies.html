01 / Mobility Uber × Metro

Connecting Urban Mobility Through Seamless Digital Ticketing

Building a unified mobility experience across five metropolitan cities.

The Vision

Urban mobility is evolving beyond individual transportation services. The future lies in connected ecosystems where commuters can seamlessly move between different modes of transport through a single digital experience.

Our team engineered the integration that enabled metro ticket booking directly through the Uber application, connecting Uber's platform with metro rail networks across five metropolitan cities. The work brought together digital mobility, ticketing infrastructure and metro operations, creating a more connected commuter experience.

What We Built

  • Metro ticket booking directly through the Uber application.
  • Seamless connectivity between Uber's platform and metro ticketing systems.
  • Integration with metro networks across five metropolitan cities.
  • Reliable backend infrastructure supporting high-volume commuter transactions.
  • Scalable technology designed to accommodate growing urban mobility demand.

The Impact

5 Metropolitan CitiesConnected metro networks across five cities through a unified digital mobility experience.
100,000 Daily Passenger CapacityInfrastructure designed to support approximately one lakh commuters per day.
One Connected Mobility EcosystemCommuters access metro ticketing through an existing digital mobility platform.
Enterprise-Grade IntegrationMultiple transportation systems connected into a seamless booking experience.

The Bigger Picture

This wasn't simply an application integration. It was the engineering of a connected mobility layer between a global digital platform and large-scale public transportation infrastructure. By bringing metro ticketing into Uber, the work helped bridge the gap between digital mobility and urban transportation — a foundation for more accessible, connected and scalable commuter experiences.

04 / Commerce Tata 1MG

Building Intelligent Inventory & Demand Management Infrastructure

Engineering inventory intelligence for high-volume digital healthcare commerce.

The Challenge

Managing inventory at scale requires more than tracking stock. Large digital healthcare platforms must continuously manage purchasing, inventory availability, demand fluctuations, replenishment decisions and operational forecasting.

With approximately 20,000 daily orders, inventory management becomes a critical component of operational efficiency. The challenge was to build technology that could support inventory visibility while enabling better purchasing and demand planning.

What We Built

Our team developed an integrated inventory management and planning system for Tata 1MG, designed to support:

Inventory Management

  • Centralized inventory tracking.
  • Inventory availability monitoring.
  • Stock management across operational workflows.

Purchase Management

  • Purchase inventory workflows.
  • Procurement-related operational management.
  • Inventory replenishment planning.

Demand Forecasting

  • Future demand prediction.
  • Inventory planning based on demand trends.
  • Forecasting capabilities to support procurement decisions.

Production & Supply Planning

  • Demand-driven production planning.
  • Inventory requirements estimation.
  • Operational planning based on projected demand.

The Impact

20,000 Daily OrdersTechnology supporting inventory and operational workflows associated with approximately 20,000 daily orders.
Intelligent Inventory ManagementCentralized inventory visibility and structured inventory management.
Predictive Demand PlanningFuture demand forecasting integrated into inventory and purchasing workflows.
Smarter Procurement OperationsPurchasing decisions supported through inventory and demand intelligence.

The Bigger Picture

Inventory intelligence is fundamental to operating high-volume commerce platforms. By integrating inventory management, purchasing, demand forecasting and production planning, the system was designed to help businesses move beyond reactive inventory management toward more intelligent operational planning.
