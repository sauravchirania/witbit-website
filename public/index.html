Frequently
asked questions

Got questions? We've got answers. If you don't find what you're looking for, feel free to reach out.

01 What does Witbit Labs actually do?+

We build AI-powered automations and custom software for businesses. That means replacing repetitive manual work with AI agents, connecting your disconnected tools, and building the internal tools your team needs — end to end, from idea to production.

02 How is this different from hiring developers?+

You get a small, senior team that thinks in AI natively — no layers of project managers, no multi-month procurement. We ship working prototypes within days and production systems in weeks, and we stay involved after launch to keep improving results.

03 What kinds of processes can be automated?+

Anything rule-based or repetitive: invoice and document processing, lead qualification, customer support, reporting, inventory updates, appointment scheduling, CRM hygiene, email workflows, and more. If your team does it more than twice a week, it's probably automatable.

04 How long does a project take?+

Focused automations: 1–2 weeks. Web apps and MVPs: 4–6 weeks. Complex, multi-system platforms: 8–12 weeks. You'll see working software at every stage — never a black box.

05 Do you work with our existing tools?+

Yes. We integrate with what you already use — HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, Slack, Google Workspace, Airtable, spreadsheets, email, WhatsApp and virtually any tool with an API. No forced migrations.

06 What happens after launch?+

We monitor, maintain and extend. Most clients start with a build engagement and continue on a monthly retainer for support, new automations and continuous optimization.

07 How do we get started?+

Send us a message below or email hello@witbitlabs.com. We'll schedule a free 30-minute discovery call to understand your workflows and outline quick wins — no lengthy sales cycles.
